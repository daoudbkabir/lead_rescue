# LeadRescue

> **Recover Revenue. Create Opportunity.**

**AI identifies. Youth recovers. Business earns.**

LeadRescue is an AI-assisted revenue recovery platform designed to help SMEs identify neglected, inactive, and high-intent leads and turn missed opportunities into recovered revenue through intelligent prioritization and personalized human follow-up.

At the same time, LeadRescue creates practical, income-generating work opportunities for university students, fresh graduates, and underemployed youth by training them as **Revenue Recovery Agents**.

---

## Problem

Businesses spend money and effort generating leads, but many potential customers are lost after the initial inquiry.

Common situations include:

- Customers ask for prices and disappear
- Interested customers receive no follow-up
- Leads become buried inside old conversations
- Follow-ups happen too late
- High-intent customers are not prioritized

At the same time, many young people struggle to find practical work opportunities that provide both income and experience.

LeadRescue connects these two problems.

---
